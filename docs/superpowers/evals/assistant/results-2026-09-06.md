## Known residual gaps (not fixed here)

- `get_automation_actionable` vs `get_pr_doctor_consolidation` (#4): tool selection is now
  reliably correct, but the model sometimes still offers to act ("Want me to consolidate?")
  after a pure read, which a strict reading of the rubric ("does not start any work") scores as
  a near-miss even though no tool was actually called.
- `report_gap` compliance (#5) improved markedly but is not 100% — an answer that offers a
  concrete workaround (e.g. "I'll send a prompt to the session instead") sometimes reads to the
  model as resolving the gap rather than one requiring it be logged too.
- `chip-ci-regressed`: occasionally still under-names the specific regressed checks despite the
  fixture enrichment in #6.

None of these are tool-routing or fixture-data bugs — the descriptions were already the
strongest lever available per the plan's guidance to tune descriptions rather than the system
prompt, and further tuning risks chasing individual judge runs rather than fixing anything
real.
